## Abstract

For decades, search engines functioned as triangulation tools: a user comparing multiple sources, noticing disagreement, and forming their own judgment. LLM-mediated search, which synthesizes ranked results into a single authoritative-sounding response, is systematically eliminating this behavior. This paper terms this elimination Triangulation Collapse (TC), formalizes the engagement-accuracy decoupling mechanism that drives it as three proven propositions, documents its consequences across four high-stakes domains (health, legal, financial, hiring), and proposes SARI (Stakes-Aware Re-ranking and Inference) to reintroduce accuracy-oriented signals into LLM search pipelines.
